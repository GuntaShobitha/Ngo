About STACKLY

We Believe Everyone Deserves a Chance to Thrive

Founded in 2008, STACKLY has grown from a small community project into a global movement. We channel resources, passion, and expertise into programs that create lasting change.

  • Transparent operations — 87% of funds go directly to programs
  • Locally-led initiatives with global support
  • Independently audited and accredited NGO
  • 32 countries, 480+ active programs in 2024
Our Story
What We Stand For

Our Core Causes

Every initiative we run is rooted in one of these four pillars that define who we are.

Education Access

Building schools, funding scholarships, and training teachers in underserved regions worldwide.

Healthcare

Mobile clinics, vaccination drives, maternal health, and disease prevention programs.

Women Empowerment

Microfinance, skills training, and safe spaces for women to build economic independence.

Environment

Reforestation, clean water access, solar energy, and sustainable agriculture initiatives.
